## Q1. Which company is it? (identification and confidence)

### Takeaway
The best match, with **high confidence**, is **REBELL AG**, a stock corporation in Steinhausen (canton Zug, Switzerland). It was founded in April 2021 for payment software, an app wallet and loyalty programmes. In 2025–2026 it was hiring to build "Europe's first Super App" (shop, pay, book, everyday tasks). It is linked to an Italian entity, **REBELL S.p.A.**, which reportedly holds an IMEL (e-money institution) licence from Banca d'Italia. It claims more than €40M raised and a launch planned for Q1 2027. Rebell Pay (Poland) is a **namesake with no link found**.

### Inferences
- REBELL AG (Swiss holding/tech company, Zug) and REBELL S.p.A. (Italian entity with the IMEL licence) are very likely the **same group**. The evidence: the same "REBELL" name, a Milan-based team (Ballabio) employed by "REBELL AG", a job posting tied to Milan, and a claimed Banca d'Italia licence. **This is inferred, not verified.** No document showing the shareholding link was found.
- The founding purpose (payment software, app wallet, loyalty, e-commerce platform) matches the job posting's description of a super app (shop, pay, book). This suggests a **payments-first** super app on the Alipay model. The Alipay, SIX Payments and Scalapay backgrounds and Guoming Cheng's possible Ant Group connection point the same way.
- The launch moved from "2026" (job posting, around Oct 2025) to "Q1 2027" (LinkedIn post, around Aug–Sep 2026). That points to a delay of at least one quarter.
- The CHF 1M debt-to-equity conversion in 2022 is the only funding visible in the Swiss register. The €40M+ figure (company claim) was most likely raised later or at the Italian entity.
- Possible link to Supertab AG (same address) is unverified. It may only be a shared fiduciary/domicile address.
